LenDen Notes

Privacy Policy

LenDen Notes is built to help you track personal lending and borrowing records simply and privately.

Last updated: 19 May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LenDen Notes handles information when you use our app or related services. LenDen Notes is designed as a simple personal record-keeping tool for tracking money you have lent, borrowed, received or paid back.

We believe personal financial notes should remain private, clear and under your control.

2. Information You Add

When using LenDen Notes, you may choose to enter details such as names, amounts, dates, due dates, notes, repayment records, currencies and related transaction information.

This information is used only to help you organise and view your own lending and borrowing records inside the app.

3. Local-First Storage

LenDen Notes is planned as a local-first app. This means your records are primarily stored on your own device, unless future optional cloud backup or sync features are introduced and enabled by you.

We do not sell your personal records or use them for advertising.

4. Future Cloud Backup or Sync

In the future, LenDen Notes may offer optional cloud backup or sync features. If introduced, these features will be optional and will be explained clearly before use.

If cloud features are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is stored, how it is protected, and how users can manage or delete their data.

5. App Permissions

LenDen Notes may request only the permissions necessary for the app to function properly. For example, future features such as reminders, exports or backups may require limited device permissions.

We aim to keep permissions minimal and relevant to the features you choose to use.

6. Analytics and Improvements

We may use basic analytics in the future to understand app performance, errors or general usage trends. Any such analytics will be used to improve the app experience and will not be used to sell personal financial information.

7. Data Security

We take privacy seriously and aim to design LenDen Notes with simple, responsible data handling practices. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure, including using device locks, passwords or other safety tools available on your phone.

8. Children’s Privacy

LenDen Notes is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LenDen Notes grows, especially when new features such as cloud backup, reminders, exports or account systems are introduced.

Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
