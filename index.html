Our story

We started in Kathmandu.

NepaliFx began in 2016 as a side hustle between a few friends and co-workers in Kathmandu, Nepal. What started as small creative work slowly became a digital creative agency helping local businesses, startups and growing companies build their presence online.

Over the years, NepaliFx helped 25+ businesses with brand identity, websites, social media, digital marketing, photography, videography, design and visual content. Sometimes we were their designers. Sometimes their marketers. Sometimes their technical support. Often, we became their creative wing.

Today, the original NepaliFx team is spread across countries and lives. But the heart of NepaliFx is still in Nepal. We are now rebuilding it with global ambition — focused on creating products and services that solve real-world problems and benefit larger communities.
